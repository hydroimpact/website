BRIDGE, the joint programme of the Swiss National Science Foundation and Innosuisse, will fund HydroImpact through its Discovery scheme. The four-year project starts in May 2027 and brings together hydrology and climate impact research at the University of Bern and the WSL Institute for Snow and Avalanche Research SLF with data science at EPFL.

Our aim is to make hydrological climate impact assessments accessible to the people who need them in practice. We will test which combinations of climate data processing and hydrological models work best for specific questions, such as changes in water availability or flood frequency, and build a cloud-based platform that runs these workflows for any catchment and reports the results with their uncertainty.

We will develop the platform together with its future users: water managers, engineering firms, hydropower operators and public administrations. If you would like to follow the project or take part in co-design workshops and early testing, [subscribe to updates]({{< relref "/" >}}#updates).
