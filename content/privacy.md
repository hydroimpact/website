This website is run by the HydroImpact project team at the University of Bern. We keep the personal data we handle to a minimum.

## Visiting the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. When you visit a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

We do not use cookies, analytics or any other tracking. Fonts are served from this website, not from third-party services.

## Email updates

If you subscribe to updates, your email address is stored by Infomaniak, which sends the emails on our behalf and stores the data in Switzerland. You first receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription.

We use your address only to send news about the project and invitations to take part in activities such as co-design workshops and early testing. We do not share it with anyone else. Every email contains a link to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

If the platform is later continued by a spin-off company founded by the project team, we will let you know before your address is transferred, and you can unsubscribe at that point.

## Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask us what data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. Write to {{< contact >}}.
