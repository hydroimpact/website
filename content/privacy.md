This website is run by the HydroImpact project team at the University of Bern. We keep the personal data we handle to a minimum.

## Visiting the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. When you visit a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

We do not use cookies, analytics or any other tracking. Fonts are served from this website, not from third-party services. The one exception is the sign-up form on the home page, which is loaded from Infomaniak's servers (see below).

## Email updates

The sign-up form on the home page is provided by Infomaniak Network SA, a Swiss company. When you open the home page, your browser loads the form from Infomaniak's servers, so Infomaniak receives technical data such as your IP address. The form sets no cookies. To block automated sign-ups it uses a spam check that runs in your browser and does not track you.

If you subscribe, Infomaniak stores your email address, and your organisation if you choose to give it, in Switzerland and sends the emails on our behalf. You first receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription. See the [Infomaniak privacy policy](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/legal/confidentiality-policy) for how Infomaniak handles data.

We use your email address only to send news about the project and invitations to take part in activities such as co-design workshops and early testing. We use your organisation, if given, only to understand who follows the project. We do not share your data with anyone else. Every email contains a link to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

If the platform is later continued by a spin-off company founded by the project team, we will let you know before your address is transferred, and you can unsubscribe at that point.
